Design a small LED driver board with copperflow.

- Power comes in as 5 V on a 2-pin 2.54 mm header (pin 1 = 5 V, pin 2 = GND).
- An AMS1117-3.3 regulator (SOT-223) makes 3.3 V, with a 10 uF capacitor on its input and one on its output.
- A green power LED with a 1 k resistor shows that 3.3 V is present.
- One red LED with its resistor is driven from 3.3 V through a second 2-pin header, so a switch can be wired to it.
- Use 0603 passives and 0603 LEDs; keep the board small, two layers.
